# TorrentXDesk

**Fast, simple torrents on your desktop.** The desktop edition of TorrentX, *an Autcust product*.

## ⬇️ Download

**[Download TorrentXDesk for Windows](https://github.com/thetitanreddy/TorrentXDesk/releases/latest/download/TorrentXDesk-Setup.exe)** (Windows 10 / 11, 64-bit)

Prefer an MSI package? [TorrentXDesk-Setup.msi](https://github.com/thetitanreddy/TorrentXDesk/releases/latest/download/TorrentXDesk-Setup.msi) · [All releases](https://github.com/thetitanreddy/TorrentXDesk/releases)

> **"Windows protected your PC"?** Windows shows this for new apps it doesn't recognise yet.
> Click **More info → Run anyway** to install.

## Features

- Magnet links, `.torrent` files and torrent links: drag and drop, or paste with Ctrl+V
- Choose which files to download and where to save them
- Watch videos while they download (sequential download + your own video player)
- Pause, resume, speed limits, download queue, seeding ratio limit
- Details for every torrent: files, peers, trackers and piece map
- Light and dark themes
- Keeps downloading in the system tray, with a notification when a download finishes
- Updates itself: you'll see a banner in the app when a new version is out

## Updating

TorrentXDesk checks for updates automatically. You can also use **Settings → Check for updates now**.
Updating keeps your torrents and settings.
